Gabriel Napier
Starboy
Level 3, Extended Diploma in Art, Design and Communication

Materials: Oil painting and spray paint

The inspiration comes from the footballer Bukayo Saka, who is the number 7 for Arsenal Football Club and England. Bukayo has had such inspiration and influence within the footballing world and is someone I idolise for the mentality and passion he possesses. This piece is all about cultural pride and power; the raw and urban graffiti, along with the celebration, captures a moment of pure joy and power.

Destination / progression: Nottingham Trent — Fine Art Degree
