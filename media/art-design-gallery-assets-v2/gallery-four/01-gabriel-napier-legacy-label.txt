Gabriel Napier
Legacy
UAL Level 3 Extended Diploma in Creative Practice, Art, Design and Communication

Materials: Spray paints, acrylic paints and oil paints

Pathway: Graphics and Illustration / Fine Art

This piece is about the rise of black movements in the 70s, where the culture of hip hop, black music, black sportsmen and significant people shaped the world and highlighted the importance of racial equality. The focus is on the footballer Pelé, who was significant in this era and is considered to be one of the most famous black people of all time.

Destination / progression: Progression: 2nd year
