Le Velours Rouge!
Level 3, Extended Diploma in Art, Design and Communication

Materials: Burgundy velvet, burgundy appliques, fringing, beads, feathers, feather boa, rigilene boning, eyelets, burgundy ribbon

This piece is a showgirl costume inspired by the Moulin Rouge. I wanted to express the liberation of women by creating a striking corset influenced by Catherine Zuber.

Destination / progression: Employment
